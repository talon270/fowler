Register — the narrowed drill

Wiktionary tags informal, slang, dated, archaic and derogatory densely, and formal almost never — 9 occurrences in 9,160 sampled entries, and zero across 59 AWL headwords. So the drill asks the question the data can answer: which of these four is marked, and therefore cannot go in an essay. A cluster where every candidate is unmarked is a broken question and is never generated.

One of these is marked. Which one stays out of academic prose

He would not be moved, and had been called for it since he was a boy.

pigheadedadjectivederogatory

Obstinate and stubborn to the point of stupidity.

“I know yeh, yeh pig-headed devil. Yeh wanta go trompin’ off with a bad hurt.”

1895 October, Stephen Crane, chapter X, in The Red Badge of Courage

Why
The other three carry no usage label. That does not make them formal — it makes them unmarked, and Wiktionary cannot tell the two apart. The claim here is only that pigheaded is marked and they are not.

The states that are not success

Loading, empty and error get designed, not left to default. No alert() and no confirm() anywhere — a modal on a validation path punishes a typo and freezes browser automation.

Skeleton matches the real card’s geometry. No spinner.

09

The decisions that took thought

Each of these came out of a measurement or a constraint, not a preference.

The accent marks errors and register with the same colour

Oxblood is the editor’s pen. A wrong answer and a word tagged derogatory are the same category of thing — something you should not have written — so they share a hue rather than competing for attention.

Neutral is drawn as an absence

The neutral chip is an outline with no fill, because the data does not assert neutrality — it just has no label. A filled green “formal” badge would be the app inventing a claim Wiktionary never made.

Nothing is centred

Split home, left-aligned reading column at 56 characters, meta on a right rail. A centred card would put the sentence’s ragged right edge in the middle of the screen and make every line start in a different place.

Fonts are embedded, not linked

Four latin-subset faces, base64, 137 KB — under 1% of the 15 MB budget. Chrome will not load a linked font file over file://, and a PWA on a phone has no Geist installed. System stacks would render this differently on every machine.

The one perpetual animation is the streak dot

It breathes on a 3.4s cycle. Everything else moves only in response to something you did, and all of it is transform and opacity. prefers-reduced-motion switches all of it off.

Estimated numbers are gold, everywhere

The finish horizon, the band count and the cluster count are all extrapolations. They render in gold with an estimated tag until the build measures them, at which point they turn into ordinary ink.
